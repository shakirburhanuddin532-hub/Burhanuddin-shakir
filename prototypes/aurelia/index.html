The Engine

01 — Ingest

Everything your house knows,
gathered into one memory.

Documents, ledgers, correspondence and the quiet knowledge of your people — absorbed into a single sovereign memory that never leaves your enclave.

02 — Reason

Thought with structure,
not statistics.
